PRIVACY NOTICE

How we use your details

Who we are. Hasta Wellness is a venture of HASWEL CARE Private Limited, 1st floor, Anjali Arcade, Technopark Phase 3 Service Road, Kazhakkoottam, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala 695583.

What we collect. When you use the care guide or an enquiry form and tick the consent box, we store your name, mobile number, the area of care you chose, your preferred timing or date, and which form you used. We do not ask for medical history, photographs, identity documents or payment details on this website — please don’t share them.

Why. Only to contact you about your request: to confirm availability, answer questions and arrange a consultation if you wish. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Where and how long. Details are stored securely with our website host, Cloudflare, and are deleted automatically after 90 days unless you become a client of the clinic. Messages you send on WhatsApp are also handled under WhatsApp’s own terms.

Job applications. If you apply for a job, we store the details you enter and any CV you upload so we can assess your application for that and similar roles. Applications and CVs are kept for 180 days and then deleted, unless you join the team. Please don’t include Aadhaar, PAN, bank details or other ID numbers.

Your choices. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw consent, at any time by calling or messaging +91 97784 58883.
