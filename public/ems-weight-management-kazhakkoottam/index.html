BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Good to know

EMS is not a substitute for a weight management plan, and it isn’t suitable for everyone. Share relevant health information — such as heart conditions, implants, pregnancy or recent surgery — directly with the clinical team during your assessment.

Suitability and outcomes vary. We won’t promise a number on the scale; we’ll help you build habits you can keep.

QUESTIONS

Good questions to ask.

What is EMS?

Electrical muscle stimulation uses gentle electrical impulses to make muscles contract during a guided session. The team will explain the format and check whether it’s suitable for you.

Can EMS help me lose weight?

EMS is not a weight-loss treatment on its own. If weight is your goal, ask about the weight management programme, which can be combined with EMS.

Is EMS safe for everyone?

No. It may not be suitable with certain heart conditions, implanted devices, pregnancy or some other health conditions. An assessment comes first.
