BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Good to know

EMS is a supervised fitness and toning format, not a medical treatment, and it isn’t suitable for everyone. It does not replace a healthy diet, activity or your doctor’s treatment for conditions such as PCOS or thyroid disease. Share relevant health information — such as heart conditions, implants, pregnancy or recent surgery — with the clinical team during your assessment.

Suitability and results vary from person to person. We won’t promise a number on the scale; we’ll measure honestly and help you build habits you can keep.

QUESTIONS

Good questions to ask.

What is EMS?

Electrical muscle stimulation uses gentle electrical impulses to make muscles contract during guided exercises. At Hasta it is a supervised fitness and toning format, not a medical treatment. You wear a wireless suit and train one-to-one with a Hasta EMS Academy-certified coach.

Why a programme and not single sessions?

Results come from consistency. A programme gives you a measured starting point, a diet guide, regular sessions, weekly check-ins and re-assessments, so you can see real change — and adjust if something isn’t working.

What does the body-composition assessment measure?

Weight, body fat percentage, muscle mass, visceral (belly) fat level, body water and metabolic rate on our analyser, plus waist, hip and other tape measurements and private progress photos. Photos are seen only by you and your care team unless you give separate permission.

How long is a session, and how often?

Sessions last 20–30 minutes depending on your programme, with at least 48 hours between sessions so your muscles can recover. Drink water before and after each session.

Can EMS help me lose weight?

EMS on its own is not a weight-loss treatment. Our programmes combine it with a diet guide, weekly accountability and habit tracking, which is where most fat loss comes from. If weight is your main goal, ask about adding the weight management programme.

I have PCOS or thyroid problems. Can I join?

Often, yes — after an assessment and alongside your doctor’s treatment. The Women’s Metabolic Health programme includes a monthly doctor review. After pregnancy, we start only when your doctor has cleared you for exercise.

Is EMS safe for everyone?

No. It isn’t suitable with a pacemaker or other implanted device, during pregnancy, with epilepsy and with some heart and other conditions. Everyone completes a health screening, a clinician’s assessment and a signed consent before the first session.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the programme and are given to you in writing after your assessment, before you commit.
