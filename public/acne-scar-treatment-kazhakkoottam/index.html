BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Before any laser session

Ask about the device, the practitioner’s experience with your skin tone, the expected benefit, alternatives, discomfort, recovery and aftercare. Risks can include pigment changes, burns or scarring, and several visits may be needed.

If a spot is new and unusual, changing, bleeding or not healing, get a medical assessment before any cosmetic removal. Painful, deep or scarring acne also deserves timely clinical care.

QUESTIONS

Good questions to ask.

Can laser treat every pimple, scar or wart?

No. Active acne, marks, scars and warts are different concerns. Acne often needs medical skin care first; laser or light treatment may be considered in selected cases after assessment.

How many sessions will I need for acne scars?

It depends on the type of scarring, your skin and the treatment chosen. You’ll get a proposed plan with session numbers, recovery time and total cost before you commit.

Is laser safe for Indian skin tones?

Suitability depends on the device, settings and your skin. Ask about the practitioner’s experience with your skin tone and whether a test area is appropriate.
