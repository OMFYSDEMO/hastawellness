BEFORE YOU DECIDE

After aligners: keeping your result

Teeth can move back after treatment, so retainers are part of the plan. Ask how long you’ll need to wear them, how often they’re replaced and what that costs.

Aligner treatment can involve temporary discomfort and needs good oral hygiene and regular reviews. Your clinician should explain the risks and alternatives before you consent.

QUESTIONS

Good questions to ask.

Are clear aligners right for everyone?

No. A dental and orthodontic assessment is needed to decide suitability and compare alternatives. Aligners need consistent wear, good oral hygiene and follow-up, and retainers afterwards.

How long does aligner treatment take?

It depends on your teeth and bite. An estimate follows your assessment — be wary of any fixed timeline given before you’ve been examined.

Can I pay for aligners in EMIs?

You can ask the clinic about Fibe healthcare finance for eligible treatment. Get your written quotation first and compare the total repayment with paying directly. Approval is not guaranteed.
