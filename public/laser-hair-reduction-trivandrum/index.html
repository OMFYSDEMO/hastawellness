BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Facial hair and hormones

New or increasing facial hair can sometimes be linked to hormones, including PCOS. Mention changes in your periods, acne or other symptoms during your consultation. Laser reduces hair; it does not treat an underlying hormonal condition, and a clinician may recommend further assessment.

Before committing, confirm exact coverage, proposed sessions, total cost, maintenance and package terms. No fixed session count, discount or permanent result is promised.

QUESTIONS

Good questions to ask.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

Laser aims for longer-term hair reduction rather than guaranteed permanent removal. A series of sessions is usually needed, and maintenance may be needed — especially for hormone-influenced facial hair.

Is laser suitable for Indian skin and fine facial hair?

It depends on the device, settings, skin tone and hair. Ask about the practitioner’s experience with your skin tone and whether a patch test is appropriate. Very fine, light, grey or white hair may respond poorly.

Does laser hair reduction hurt?

Most people feel some warmth or snapping, and temporary redness is common. Burns, pigment changes and scarring are possible, which is why suitability, settings and aftercare matter.
