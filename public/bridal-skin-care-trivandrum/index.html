BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Before you book a package

Ask for a written breakdown of the proposed treatments, number of sessions, spacing, home-care recommendations, inclusions, exclusions and total price — plus any package validity or cancellation terms.

Leave time before important events. Some treatments need recovery time, and a new treatment right before a wedding or shoot isn’t always wise.

QUESTIONS

Good questions to ask.

When should I start pre-bridal skin care?

Ideally a few months before your wedding, so there’s time to assess your skin, plan visits and allow recovery. Shorter timelines are possible but limit the options.

Is bridal skin care only for brides?

No — grooms and family members are welcome too. The plan is built around your skin and your dates.

What does a skin package cost?

It depends on the treatments and sessions proposed after your consultation. You’ll receive the total cost in writing before you decide.
