BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Looking at the whole picture

Your clinician may suggest targeted tests — such as a blood count, iron status, thyroid function or selected vitamins — when your history warrants them. Sleep, stress and routine are part of the conversation too; relaxation support helps wellbeing but is not a hair-regrowth treatment.

Sudden, patchy or painful hair loss, or an inflamed scalp, needs prompt clinical assessment.

QUESTIONS

Good questions to ask.

Is PRP or GFC right for every type of hair loss?

No. Diagnosis comes first. PRP may be considered for selected hair-loss conditions; GFC protocols and evidence vary. Neither guarantees regrowth, and procedures can involve pain, bruising, bleeding or infection.

Do I need blood tests for hair fall?

Not automatically. Tests depend on your history and examination, and are recommended only when they’re likely to help.

How many PRP sessions are needed?

There’s no fixed number. A session and review plan is proposed after assessment, with costs in writing before you book a course.
